Bedroom Interiors

A Room Planned Around Rest

Bedrooms are planned around comfort and everyday function first - bed placement, side units, reading light and storage - before wall treatments and finishes are decided. The goal is a room that feels calm and uncluttered, not just styled for a photograph.

Wardrobes, dressing units and lighting are coordinated with the rest of the room, so storage stays out of the way of daily routines.
