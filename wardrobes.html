Wardrobes & Storage

Storage Planned to Fit, Not Squeezed In

Wardrobes are planned around what actually needs to be stored — clothing, linen, luggage and everyday accessories — before shutters, hardware or finishes are chosen. Lofts and vanity units are added where they make sense for the room, not as an afterthought.

Glossy laminates, integrated mirror units and TV niches can all be built into the same wardrobe run, keeping the bedroom's storage coordinated as one piece rather than several.

Freestanding & Built-In

Options for Every Room

From full-height built-in wardrobes to freestanding wood-finish units, storage is chosen to suit the room's layout, ceiling height and how much wall space is available.
